# Your website — how it works, and how to change it

**Your site:** https://main-website-x1jn.vercel.app

Everything on the front of the site is now yours to change, directly, by asking
for it in plain English. No waiting on anyone, no feedback forms, no lists of
requests. You describe the change and it happens.

---

## Try it first — five minutes

Do this before reading the rest. It's the fastest way to understand what you
now have.

1. Go to **claude.ai** and sign in
2. Open **Claude Code** (in the sidebar, or **claude.ai/code**)
3. Choose **Connect GitHub** and give it access to **LottusAttune/main-website**
4. Type:

> Read CLAUDE.md first. Then on the Benefits page, change the heading
> "Rest & Restore" to "Rest & Recover".

Watch it happen. Refresh your site a minute later and it's there.

That's the whole system. Everything below is detail.

## What you can change this way

Anything you can see on the site:

- **Words** — any heading, any paragraph, any button
- **Photographs** — swap one for another, add new ones, change which page they
  sit on
- **Layout** — make a section taller, move it above another, add a new one
- **Look** — spacing, sizes, how something reads on a phone

Ask the way you'd ask a designer:

> The Offerings page feels cramped. Give the two boxes more room.

> Use a different photo on the Founder page — the one with the crystal bowls.

> Add a fourth point under For Individuals, about sleep.

> The heading on What is Included is too big on my phone.

**Worth saying at the start of any session:** *"Read CLAUDE.md first."* That
file holds the rules for your site — your words are never reworded without
asking, no AI-generated images ever, and photographs are handled properly so
your pages stay fast.

### Changes are live straight away

This is your site, so there's no approval step. A change is on the website
within a minute or two.

Two habits make that comfortable:

- **Ask to see it first** — *"Show me how that looks before you save it."*
- **One thing at a time** — small separate changes are far easier to undo

### Undoing anything

> Undo that last change.

> Put the Founder page back how it was yesterday.

Every version is saved permanently. Nothing is ever really lost.

## The dashboard — the business side

**https://main-website-x1jn.vercel.app/studio**

This is the part you'll use weekly, and it needs no Claude at all.

**Pricing & availability**
: Set the private session rate, the package of four, the per-participant group
  rate, the team-building add-on, refreshments and the deposit. Press **Publish
  to website** and the new prices appear everywhere on the site at once — the
  Offerings page, the booking form, the gift calculator. You never have to
  change a price in more than one place.

**Availability**
: Turn the 7am, midday or evening session on or off. Click any date on the
  calendar to close it — it greys out on the booking page immediately. Set how
  far ahead people must book (currently five days).

**Discount codes**
: Four exist (WELCOME20, WELCOME30, LOTUS20, LOTUS30). Pause or activate each
  one. They apply to group bookings of two or more.

**Leads**
: Every booking enquiry, on a board: New enquiry → Contacted → Proposal sent →
  Booked → Complete. Drag a card along as things progress, so you always know
  who is waiting on you.

**Bookings, Gift cards, Clients**
: Confirmed sessions, gift certificates issued, and everyone who has ever
  booked with their lifetime value. Each exports to Excel with one button.

## How the whole thing fits together

Useful to understand once:

- **The website** is what your clients see
- **The dashboard** is where you run the business
- **The database** quietly holds the real information — every booking request,
  gift request, your prices and your closed dates

When someone requests a session, it goes into the database and appears in your
Leads board. When you change a price, it goes into the same database and the
website reads the new figure. That's why a change in the dashboard shows up on
the site instantly, and why a booking made on someone's phone in the evening is
waiting for you in the morning.

Nothing lives only in one browser. Nothing is lost if a laptop dies.

## Your own domain

Right now the site lives at a temporary address. To put it on
**lotusattune.com** (or whichever you own):

1. Buy the domain if you haven't — Namecheap, GoDaddy or Google Domains
2. In **Vercel**, open the project → **Settings → Domains** → add it
3. Vercel shows you two or three DNS records to add at whoever sold you the
   domain. Copy them across exactly.
4. Wait — usually minutes, occasionally a few hours

The certificate that makes it `https://` is set up automatically. Nothing about
the site needs to change.

Nevin can do this with you on a call; it's the one part where a wrong character
means it silently doesn't work.

## If something looks wrong

1. Refresh properly first — **Cmd + Shift + R**. Browsers hold on to old
   copies, and that's the cause more often than not.
2. Still wrong? Tell Claude which page, what you see, and what you expected.
3. Made it worse? *"Something's broken — put the site back to how it was before
   today's changes."*

Nothing you do can permanently break this.

## One thing that isn't yours to change

The **prices in the code**, the **booking form's inner workings** and anything
asking for a **password or key**. Prices are set in the dashboard instead, so
the figure a client sees and the figure you're paid can never disagree. If a
change seems to need a password, ask Nevin.

Claude knows this and will tell you rather than doing it quietly.
